How to install

Two minutes, once.

Plate isn’t on the Play Store yet, so Android asks you to confirm the install. That’s normal for apps downloaded from a website.

Tap “Download for Android”

On your Android phone. If Chrome asks whether to keep the file, tap Download anyway.

Open the downloaded file

Tap the notification, or find Plate.apk in your Downloads.

Allow installs from your browser

Android shows “For your security, your phone isn’t allowed to install unknown apps from this source.” Tap Settings, switch on Allow from this source, then go back.

Tap Install, then Open

Plate is new and not on the Play Store yet, so Google Play Protect may not recognise it. If it warns you, tap More details → Install anyway.

✓ After that, Plate tells you when a new version is out: an Update badge appears in the app and brings you back here. Updates keep your food diary.
Installed Plate before version 2.0? Version 2.0 is signed with Plate’s new security key, so Android can’t install it over an older Plate. Uninstall your current Plate first (this clears its diary), then install 2.0. Future updates install normally.
